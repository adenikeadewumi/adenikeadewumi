### About Me

I'm an Electronic and Electrical Engineering student at
Obafemi Awolowo University, Nigeria, interested in building
intelligent systems that can perceive, learn, make decisions
and interact with the physical world.

My interests lie at the intersection of **artificial intelligence,
computer vision and robotics**, particularly in how perception,
learning and control can be integrated to help robots understand
their surroundings and respond appropriately.

I enjoy exploring both the computational and engineering sides
of intelligent systems, from developing machine learning models
to integrating them with hardware and robotic platforms.

### Areas of Interest

- Robot learning and intelligent robotic systems
- Computer vision and visual perception
- Sensor fusion and perception-to-action systems
- Adaptive control and robotic manipulation
- Edge AI and efficient model deployment
- Machine learning and deep learning
